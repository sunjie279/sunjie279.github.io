I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate at USTC, jointly trained at Shanghai Innovation Institute. My research focuses on LLM post-training, spanning agent trajectory and environment curation, on-policy distillation, and test-time enhancement. I have authored 8 first- or co-first-author papers and conducted research internships at Tencent and Ant Group.

I am happy to chat about research and collaborations. Reach me at sunjie2019@mail.ustc.edu.cn.
